Who We Are

BUILDING CHAMPIONS
SINCE 1993

The Pencak Silat Association of Sundargarh (PSAS) is a premier martial arts organization dedicated to the development and promotion of Pencak Silat and other martial arts in Sundargarh district, Odisha. With a legacy spanning over three decades, PSAS has produced national and international champions.

Recognized by the Government of India and affiliated with national and international federations, PSAS represents the pinnacle of martial arts training in eastern India.

Our Vision

To be India's leading martial arts institution, producing world-class athletes for Olympic and World Championship levels.

Our Mission

To empower youth through disciplined training, fostering physical fitness, mental resilience, and competitive excellence.
