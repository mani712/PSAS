Who We Are

BUILDING CHAMPIONS
SINCE 1993

Pencak Silat Association of Sundargarh (PSAS) is the sole governing body for the sport of Pencak Silat in the Sundargarh district. Our legitimacy and national integration are affirmed through a robust affiliation structure recognized by Government of India.

Our Vision

To establish Pencak Silat as a premier sport in Sundargarh, nurturing a generation of disciplined, fit, and morally upright individuals who can achieve national and international glory.

Our Mission

To promote, develop, and structure the art of Pencak Silat across Sundargarh district by providing high-quality training, identifying and grooming talent, and creating a clear pathway from district games to the world stage.

About The Art

ABOUT THE ART: PENCAK SILAT

Pencak Silat is a complete martial art and cultural heritage originating from the Indonesian-Malay archipelago. It is more than a combat sport; it is a system for holistic development.

COMPLETE MARTIAL ART & CULTURAL HERITAGE
Originating from Indonesian-Malay archipelago — sport, fitness, artistic forms & traditional martial art

A RICH HERITAGE

Traditionally, Silat was an integral part of a young person's education, forging character and discipline on the path to adulthood.

HOLISTIC DEVELOPMENT

Silat enhances self-awareness, discipline, integrity, responsibility, and cooperation. Under qualified teachers, students develop physically, mentally, morally, and spiritually.

VERSATILE PRACTICE

It can be pursued for sport and fitness, for the aesthetic beauty of its artistic forms, or as a complete traditional martial art.
